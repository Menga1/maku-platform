2. What MAKU is

MAKU is an occupational health and safety (HSE) decision-support tool for construction and engineering sites. It estimates environmental risk (heat stress, wind, air quality, electrical and noise hazards) from measurements and published methods. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat anyone.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, consent and assessment data: until you delete your account or your organisation closes its MAKU workspace.
  • Sign-in attempt records: 7 days.
  • Security audit log: [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 24 months], then deleted. When an account is deleted, its audit entries are kept for this period because they are needed to investigate security incidents.
  • Database backups: 14 days, then overwritten.

6. Your rights

  • Access and portability: My account → Export my data (JSON file).
  • Withdraw consent: My account → switch off AI briefings or Location at any time.
  • Deletion: My account → Delete my account, or see maku.ae/legal/delete-account.
  • Correction, restriction, objection: email privacy@maku.ae. We reply within 30 days.
  • Complaints: you may complain to the UAE Data Office or, in the EU/EEA, to your local data-protection authority.

7. Security

HTTPS everywhere, bcrypt password hashing, server-side lockout after repeated failed sign-ins, role-based access, a tamper-evident audit log, and encrypted nightly backups.

8. Children

MAKU is a workplace tool for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18.

9. Changes

When this policy changes in a way that affects consent, MAKU asks you to confirm your choices again before you continue.
