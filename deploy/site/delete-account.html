Delete your MAKU account

MAKU is published by an individual developer based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. You can delete your MAKU account and its personal data at any time, in the app or by email.

Option 1 - in the app (immediate)

  1. Sign in to MAKU (app or the MAKU web app).
  2. Open My account from the menu.
  3. Scroll to Delete my account, type your username and password, and press Delete permanently.

Deletion happens immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 - by email (if you no longer have the app)

Send a request from the email address linked to your account, or include your username:

Email a deletion request

We verify that the request comes from the account holder and complete it within 30 days, usually within 3 working days. We confirm by email when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: username, email, role and password hash
  • Your privacy choices (consent records)
  • Every risk assessment you ran
  • Your sign-in attempt records

What is kept, and for how long

  • Security audit-log entries (for example sign-in times) are kept for 24 months to investigate security incidents, then deleted.
  • Site-level safety alerts that are not linked to your account remain with your organisation.
  • A deletion receipt containing only a one-way hash of your username and the date, as proof the deletion happened.
  • Our database provider keeps a restore history that rolls over within 6 hours.
