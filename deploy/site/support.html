Common questions

I'm locked out after several wrong passwords. Wait 15 minutes, then try again. If you have forgotten your password, contact your MAKU administrator.

Why do I see a red "SIMULATED DATA" banner? The deployment is in training/demo mode, or a live data feed is unavailable. Do not use those numbers for site decisions - measure on site.

The AI briefing is missing. AI briefings are optional. Turn them on under My account → Privacy and consent.

How do I delete my account? See the account-deletion page.

Important

MAKU is a decision-support tool. It does not replace a competent person, a permit-to-work system, an IEEE 1584 arc-flash study, or a certified engineering assessment. In an emergency, stop work and follow your site emergency procedure.
