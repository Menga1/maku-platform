**Full description (both stores)**
```
MAKU helps HSE managers, site supervisors and engineers screen environmental and physical risks on construction and engineering sites - before work starts and while it is under way.

Enter readings from your instruments, or use live weather where available. MAKU applies documented, published methods and the limits of your country, then shows a clear risk level, the governing hazard, the controls to apply and when work should stop.

EIGHT SITE ENVIRONMENTS
- Solar farms: heat stress from air temperature, solar irradiance, UV and ground reflection
- Offshore: Humidex and wind limits for deck work and lifting
- Tunnels and metros: heat, carbon monoxide and PM2.5 against exposure limits
- High-rise: wind at working height for crane and facade operations
- Data centres: arc-flash incident energy (Lee method), hot/cold-aisle temperatures and confined-space conditions
- Wind energy: hub-height wind, lightning (30-30 rule) and sea state for crew transfer
- Mining and quarrying: respirable silica, daily noise dose and whole-body vibration
- Marine and port: tide clearance, night-time visibility and splash-zone corrosion

BUILT FOR REAL SITES
- Country rules for the USA, UK, France, UAE, Canada and Australia, plus a global reference profile
- Initial and residual risk on a 5x5 matrix, with the controls credited to each hazard
- Clear stop-work alerts when a limit is reached
- PDF and HTML reports recording inputs, method version and evidence
- Organisations with viewer, supervisor and admin roles - each organisation's data is kept separate
- Works in 14 languages, including English, French and Arabic
- Optional written briefing generated by AI (off unless you turn it on; it never changes a risk result)

IMPORTANT
MAKU is a decision-support and screening tool. It does not replace a competent person, a permit-to-work system, an IEEE 1584 arc-flash study, measurements on site or a certified engineering assessment. It is not a medical device. In an emergency, stop work and follow your site emergency procedure.

An account is required. Accounts are created by your organisation's MAKU administrator.
```

Apple and Google need a working sign-in. Create a **reviewer account** with the supervisor role in a test organisation, give its username and password in these notes, and remove it after approval:

```
MAKU is a workplace HSE risk-screening tool for construction sites. Accounts are issued by each organisation's administrator, so please use this test account:
Username: <reviewer username>
Password: <reviewer password>

After signing in, open any module from the menu (for example "Solar Farms"), keep the default values and tap "Run Risk Assessment" to see the risk level, controls and report. Location and AI briefings are optional and can be left off.
```
